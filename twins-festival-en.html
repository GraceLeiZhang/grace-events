Every May Day holiday · The “Home of Twins” on the Tropic of Cancer · Twins from all over the world

Mojiang in Yunnan is known as the “Home of Twins”. Every May Day holiday it hosts the International Twins Festival, and twins from around the world come to celebrate 🎉
Grace Rock is bringing Sydney twin families together: the kids parade, perform and make friends with thousands of other twins, while parents enjoy the Tropic of Cancer, local tea and like-minded families.

📌 This page is updated as details are confirmed.

🎁 Perks for twins (from the organisers)

  • 🆓 Free registration: just fill in the twins' details, no ID number needed
  • 🎟️ Free entry to scenic spots for twins, plus 1 guardian free (Twins Culture Park ¥20, Tropic of Cancer Park ¥40, Confucian Temple ¥20)
  • 🏨 Twins' hotel agreed rate (this year: standard room from ¥280), shared with family in the same room
  • 🎁 Welcome pack and free festival activities
  • 🎤 Talent twins invited to perform at the gala get local hosting and a travel subsidy
  • 🍜 Other meals and accommodation are self-funded, to suit any budget

Offers are agreed each year between the organisers, scenic spots and hotels, and published in advance (with the registration link) on the Mojiang Culture & Tourism and Mojiang International Twins Association WeChat accounts.

  • 🍵 Hani culture and tea country: Hani customs, tea gardens, Yunnan home cooking and rice noodles
  • 🌸 Kunming (optional): Stone Forest, Dianchi Lake, flower market; Xishuangbanna can be added
🛡️ Safety
  • Each child is supervised by their own parent throughout
  • Each family should buy travel insurance covering the trip
  • The final itinerary is confirmed before departure
📋 Before you go
  • Passports need at least 6 months' validity
  • Australian passport holders can currently visit China visa-free; please re-check before travel
  • Families with other passports should check their own visa requirements
  • Twins register by filling in their details; we will share the link when it opens
  • Mojiang in May is warm by day and cool at night: bring sun protection and a jacket
