Crossing the Northern Tropic
From Shanghai to Yunnan: April Holiday Family Adventure 🌈

April 2027 school holidays · Shanghai · Xishuangbanna Water Festival · Tropical Botanical Garden (5A) · Northern Tropic · the mysterious Ailao Mountains · Dian Lake, Pearl of the Plateau

Start in Shanghai, fly south to the rainforests of Xishuangbanna, then head north, cross the Northern Tropic (23.5°N, where the summer sun shines straight overhead) in Mojiang, reach Kunming, the City of Eternal Spring, and fly home via Guangzhou 🧭
Go up China’s tallest building and explore the gaming and pop-culture side of Shanghai; then join a real Dai New Year Water-Splashing Festival in Xishuangbanna. No need to fight over water pistols: the whole city is the battlefield, and the wetter you get, the more blessings you receive 💦
Explore the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Tropical Botanical Garden (a national 5A site) and see the rainforest from the textbooks; be little farmers in Mojiang, picking tea, catching fish and planting rice, and make friends at a local high school; then walk into the misty old-growth forest of the Ailao Mountains and see Huayao Dai embroidery. In Kunming: the Stone Forest, a hot spring, grass sledding, laser tag and Asia’s biggest flower market 🌺 And a day in Guangzhou on the way home for proper dim sum 🥟

📌 The itinerary may change slightly depending on flights, weather and local conditions; the final version will be confirmed before departure.

🗓️ Itinerary
  • Sat 10 Apr Evening departure from Sydney (China Eastern, 9:30 pm) ✈️
  • 🏙️ Shanghai: tech, gaming and Chinese pop culture
  • Sun 11 Apr Old Shanghai and neon nights: land early and rest at the hotel. In the afternoon, go up to the 118th-floor observation deck of Shanghai Tower, China’s tallest building, for views over the Huangpu River 🏙️ At dusk, take the 2-yuan ferry across to the Bund to watch the old waterfront light up, then stroll Nanjing Road and try the latest tech at the Apple and Huawei flagship stores
    🍽️ Free meal
  • Mon 12 Apr Chinese pop culture and anime: morning at Yu Garden, which often has game-themed stamp trails – the kids go treasure-hunting with a map while the adults admire the architecture and eat Nanxiang soup dumplings 🥟 Afternoon at Bailian ZX, a whole building of anime, figurines, trading cards and VR games 🎮 Dinner in the lanes of Xintiandi or Tianzifang
    🍽️ Free meal
  • 🐘 Xishuangbanna: the Water Festival
  • Tue 13 Apr Ride the Maglev to Pudong Airport at 300 km/h 🚄 then fly direct to Xishuangbanna. We stay right next to Water-Splashing Square. In the evening, release sky lanterns by the Lancang River and browse the Gaozhuang night market 🏮
    🍽️ Free evening at the night market – leaf-wrapped grilled meats, bamboo-tube rice, pineapple rice
  • Wed 14 Apr Morning at Wild Elephant Valley to see Asian elephants 🐘 Afternoon at the Primeval Forest Park to watch the peacocks take flight 🦚, and dress up in Dai clothes for photos
    🍽️ Peacock Feast: a Dai banquet laid out on banana leaves like a peacock’s tail – lemongrass fish, Dai roast chicken, purple rice (no peacocks involved 😄)
  • Thu 15 Apr Festival day 💦 At dawn, the river water-collecting ceremony: young women draw holy water from the Mother River and the blessings begin. Then step outside into Water-Splashing Square and join the whole city for a day-long water fight!
    🍽️ Dai home cooking after the splashing
  • Fri 16 Apr The Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanical Garden (a national 5A site): the poisonous “arrow-poison tree”, giant water lilies strong enough to hold a child, dancing grass and strangler figs – the rainforest from the textbooks 🌿 It’s huge, so we ride the electric carts
    🍽️ Free meal
  • 🌾 Mojiang · 🌲 Ailao Mountains
  • Sat 17 Apr Early bullet train to Mojiang (the only train of the trip); afternoon spring tea picking and fishing in a muddy pond 🐟
    🍽️ Hani village feast: Mojiang purple rice, plus the fish we caught that day; then dance around a bonfire with our Hani hosts 🔥
  • Sun 18 Apr Rice planting; a costume workshop to see embroidery, batik and tie-dye, and tie-dye your own handkerchief 🎨; the Northern Tropic park, with its rainbow “flying carpet” slide and planetarium ☀️ Then a sip from the Twins Well – legend says it brings twins 😄
    🍽️ Twins Feast: Mojiang is the “City of Twins”, and every May Day it hosts an international twins festival with more than a thousand pairs of twins from around the world 👯 One day we’ll come back for it!
  • Mon 19 Apr Morning visit to a local high school (see below) 🤝 After lunch, a short hop to Bixi Old Town for a stroll along an old Tea Horse Road street, then the private bus on the expressway to Gasa, at the foot of the Ailao Mountains (about 2 hours)
    🍽️ Free evening meal
  • Tue 20 Apr Ailao Mountains: walk into misty old-growth forest draped in moss, like a fairy tale 🌲 See a waterfall, visit a Huayao Dai village and their heritage embroidery
    🍽️ Huayao Dai feast
  • Wed 21 Apr Morning at the Longxi family manor, then back to Kunming (about 3 hours); we stay by Green Lake
    🍽️ Steam-pot chicken
  • 🌸 Kunming (staying by Green Lake)
  • Thu 22 Apr Old Kunming: Green Lake Park → Yunnan Military Academy (where future marshals Zhu De and Ye Jianying studied) → the former campus of Southwest Associated University, China’s famous wartime university – all within walking distance. Afternoon at Grand View Tower to see the “longest couplet in China” – 180 characters. Which kid can memorise it? 😄
    🍽️ Old Kunming street food: erkuai rice cakes and tofu-pudding rice noodles
  • Fri 23 Apr The Stone Forest: a seabed 270 million years ago, now a forest of rock 🪨 On the way back, soak in a hot spring at Yangzonghai Lake ♨️, then back to Green Lake for the night
    🍽️ Yiliang roast duck
  • Sat 24 Apr The Dragon Gate cliff path in the Western Hills, looking over Dian Lake; afternoon at Dounan Flower Market 🌺
    🍽️ Flower Feast: dishes cooked with edible flowers – and flower cakes to take home

    🍽️ Mushroom feast for lunch

  • Mon 26 Apr Morning dim sum 🫖, then Shamian Island and the Chen Clan Ancestral Hall in the old Lingnan city; at dusk, the Pearl River and Canton Tower. 9 pm flight home to Sydney

🌾 Mojiang: little farmers
  • Pick spring tea and see how leaves on a bush become tea in your cup 🍵
  • Catch fish in a muddy pond. Clothes won’t survive – bring a spare set 🐟
  • Plant rice in the paddies with local farmers (depending on the season)
  • Visit a local costume workshop to see how Hani clothing is made – embroidery, batik and tie-dye. Kids can tie-dye their own handkerchief to take home 🎨
  • Mojiang is a Hani autonomous county, so kids can buy traditional Hani clothes and wear them around the village 👗
  • Northern Tropic park: stand right on the line for a photo, ride the rainbow “flying carpet” slide down the hill and visit the planetarium ☀️
  • Make friends and learn from each other at a local high school: sit in on a class; each child brings an English book they’ve read as a gift for a new friend 📚; our kids share Aboriginal dot painting, they share the Hani rice terraces, and together we paint one picture 🎨; our kids teach English, they teach Chinese; play table tennis, soccer and basketball together 🏓⚽🏀, chess and calligraphy, and Rubik’s cube fans can show others a few tricks 🧩
  • Grace has already been in touch with the Mojiang tourism bureau. This trip is a first look: the plan is to grow Mojiang into a longer rural and school exchange, and you’ll be the very first families to try it 🌟
🌲 Ailao Mountains: misty old-growth forest
  • A national nature reserve with ancient moss-covered forest, often wrapped in cloud – like stepping into a fairy tale
  • Gasa and Mosha, in the Red River valley below, are home to the Huayao (Flowery Waist) Dai: heritage embroidery and authentic Dai food
  • A waterfall and the century-old Longxi family manor
  • We take the expressway from Mojiang and back to Kunming – no winding mountain roads

📋 Before you go

  • Australian passport holders can currently visit China visa-free – please check the latest rules before departure

  • Each family should take out travel insurance
