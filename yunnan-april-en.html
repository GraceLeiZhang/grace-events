Crossing the Northern Tropic
From Shanghai to Yunnan: April Holiday Family Adventure 🌈

April 2027 school holidays · Shanghai · Xishuangbanna Water Festival · Tropical Botanical Garden (5A) · Northern Tropic · the mysterious Ailao Mountains · Dian Lake, Pearl of the Plateau

Start in Shanghai, fly south to the rainforests of Xishuangbanna, then head north, cross the Northern Tropic (23.5°N, where the summer sun shines straight overhead) in Mojiang, reach Kunming, the City of Eternal Spring, and fly home via Guangzhou 🧭
Go up China’s tallest building and explore the gaming and pop-culture side of Shanghai; then join a real Dai New Year Water-Splashing Festival in Xishuangbanna. No need to fight over water pistols: the whole city is the battlefield, and the wetter you get, the more blessings you receive 💦
Explore the Chinese Academy of Sciences’ Tropical Botanical Garden (a national 5A site) and see the rainforest from the textbooks; be little farmers in Mojiang, picking tea, catching fish and planting rice, and make friends at a local high school (proposed); then walk into the misty old-growth forest of the Ailao Mountains and see Huayao Dai embroidery. In Kunming: the Stone Forest, a hot spring, grass sledding, laser tag and Asia’s biggest flower market 🌺 And a day in Guangzhou on the way home for proper dim sum 🥟

📌 The itinerary may change slightly depending on flights, weather and local conditions; the final version will be confirmed before departure.

🇨🇳 Friends coming from within China are welcome to meet us in Shanghai, Xishuangbanna or Kunming.

🧭 Highlights
  • 🏙️ Shanghai: tech and culture: the view over the Huangpu River, gaming and pop-culture Shanghai, a ride on the maglev 🚄
  • 💦 Xishuangbanna Water Festival: a real Dai New Year, where the whole city splashes and the wetter you get, the more blessings you receive
  • 🐘 Dai culture: the Tropical Botanical Garden (5A), Dai food and costume; sky lanterns by the river (proposed, subject to local rules and weather)
  • ☀️ Mojiang: the Tropic of Cancer and twin culture: stand on the line where the summer sun is directly overhead, and hear the stories of the “City of Twins”; tea picking and fishing (subject to the farming season and local conditions)
  • 🌲 Ailao Mountains: misty old-growth forest draped in moss, and Huayao Dai heritage embroidery
  • 🌸 Kunming and Yunnan food: the Stone Forest, Dian Lake, the Dounan flower market; steam-pot chicken, rice noodles, wild mushrooms and flower cakes
  • 🤝 Family and cross-cultural exchange: an exchange with a local school (proposed, subject to the school’s arrangements) where kids share Australia and Yunnan, draw, play sport and try calligraphy together
🛡️ Safety and supervision

📋 Before you go
  • Passports need at least 6 months’ validity
  • Australian passport holders can currently visit China visa-free; please check the latest rules before travelling
  • Families on Chinese or other passports should check their own visa and return-to-Australia documents
  • Xishuangbanna is hot in April, so bring sun protection and hats; Kunming is cool in the mornings and evenings, so bring a jacket
  • For the Water Festival: a waterproof phone pouch, sandals and at least two changes of clothes
