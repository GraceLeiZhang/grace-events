2. Member points rules

  1. Free to joinNo joining fee and no package you have to buy.
  2. One member ID for lifeFormat GR-C-AU-0001. Businesses get GR-S and volunteers GR-V. IDs are issued by Grace Rock.
  3. Points come only from real purchasesAt businesses in the program, $1 spent = 1 point (unless a business page says otherwise).
  4. Referrals count only after a real purchaseSign-up without a purchase = 0 points. We never reward recruiting on its own. The number of referral levels and their share are still in trial and will be published on this page.
  5. Using pointsFrom 100 points, use them at any Grace Rock partner business in the program.
  6. Points are not money, but they never expireNo cash-out and not transferable. Once you join, your membership and points are kept for good and never cleared. If you are away for a long time you are just marked inactive, and everything is back when you return. We promise no income or returns.
  7. Funded by partner businessesEach business in the program agrees its contribution in writing. Points used across businesses are settled monthly, and the accounts are open to the businesses in the program.
  8. The founder takes no pointsGrace Lei Zhang personally receives no points or referral benefits. Points at the top of a referral chain go to the Grace Rock company.
  9. Your privacyYour details are kept under Australia's Privacy Act 1988, used only for member services, and who referred whom is never made public.
  10. Changes are announced firstRules may change during the trial. Any change is posted on this page before it takes effect.
