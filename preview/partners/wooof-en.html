About WOOOF

WOOOF Dog Grooming is a premium boutique grooming salon in Surry Hills, bringing a calm, high-quality grooming experience to dogs across Sydney’s inner city.

Our award-winning groomers provide full-service grooming for all breeds and sizes, including bathing, clipping, hand scissoring, hand stripping, breed-standard styling and de-shedding, along with therapeutic spa treatments.

Skin and coat health is at the heart of everything we do: each dog is groomed individually in a relaxed, low-stress environment, using natural products selected to nourish the skin and keep coats in top condition. Whether your dog needs a regular maintenance groom or a full makeover, our focus is always on healthy, happy dogs. Bookings are available online or by phone.
