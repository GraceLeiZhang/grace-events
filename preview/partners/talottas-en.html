Grace’s review

A local shop with real character. This time we tried the burgers, prawn, vegetarian, meat and more, and even took some home for our teenagers. They taste amazing! Best eaten fresh and hot, so go and enjoy them warm in the shop.

It’s on Bridge St in Kurnell, minutes from Kamay National Park: perfect after a bush walk, a day by the sea or whale watching (whale season is roughly May to November). The pizzas are named after classic cars: Falcon GT, Monaro, Torana, Mustang and more.

—— Grace Lei Zhang

The owner’s giving story

On the shop wall hangs an old newspaper story, “Truckies deliver tonnes of Christmas joy”. Ever since opening his pizzeria in the Sutherland Shire, owner Tony Talotta has given back with his “toy for a taste” idea: bring a toy to donate and get a free small pizza when you dine.

The toys go to children in hospital. One Christmas he and his mate Bruce Lawson hired a truck, filled it with toys and drove about 1,550 km to Taree and Wagga Wagga, for families and children affected by bushfires and in need.

— From the newspaper clipping in the shop

Inside: a retro garage

Vinyl records on the ceiling, old number plates, oil cans, model cars and sports memorabilia on the walls, and a “gladiator” at the door. Air-conditioned, with a big screen TV.
