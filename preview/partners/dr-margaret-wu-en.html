About

About
Dr Margaret Wu (Xiaoqin Wu) is a highly experienced Chinese medicine practitioner, academic and researcher. She holds a Bachelor of Medicine and Master of Medical Science from Beijing University of Chinese Medicine and a PhD from the University of Technology Sydney. She worked as a medical oncologist at the China–Japan Friendship Hospital, has practised as a registered Chinese medicine practitioner in Australia since 2007, is a Practitioner Member of the Chinese Medicine Council NSW, and teaches and supervises at Australian universities. She consults at Myhealth Westmead (NICM Health Research Institute building).
