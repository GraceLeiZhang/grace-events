Her story

Story

Shan Jianghong grew up in Donghai, Jiangsu, known as the world's crystal capital. In primary school she won the county's top prize for illustrated stories, and art stayed with her ever since.

In 2000 she used her savings to start a crystal jewellery workshop, and the same year entered Yangzhou University to study environmental art design, graduating in 2003. Today she chairs Songsheng Crystal Art & Culture Co., leading original designs that pair crystal with precious metals.

Over almost three years she wrote Jing Cheng Gu Yun (晶呈古韵), sharing 30+ years of knowledge about crystal's origins, craft and art.
