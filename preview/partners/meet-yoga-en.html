My experience

Grace Lei Zhang

On Saturday 24 October I went to my first proper class with Grace He, a free trial.

What stayed with me most was the small class. She watches each person closely and gives individual guidance, correcting our poses one by one.

In that very first class she noticed how stiff my shoulders and neck were. After nearly 30 years of computer work I have picked up some poor posture habits, and they have bothered me for years. I am grateful that she spotted it so quickly; it made me realise how much good posture matters.

I moved my whole body, had a good sweat, and came away more confident about keeping up yoga.

— Grace Lei Zhang · Founder, Grace Rock
