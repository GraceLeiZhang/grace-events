From Grace

A friend recently recommended an Australian wine to me: the Blue River range from Calabria Family Wines, a family winery making wine in Australia since 1945.

I haven't tasted it myself yet, so I won't call it tried and tested. What made me happy to share it is the independent reviews: critics at The Real Review, including Huon Hooke and Stuart Knox, have recommended these wines. Nice for Christmas, New Year parties and gifts.

— Grace Lei Zhang
