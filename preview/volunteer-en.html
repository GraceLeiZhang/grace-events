How to earn Good Points

  • Join an activity +10Helping, photos, translating, welcoming guests all count.
  • Lead or organise one +20
  • Full-day major event +30

A thank-you per activity, not per hour, and not a wage.

A permanent, clear record

  • 📒 Every activity recordedDate, activity and points go into the ledger. Entries are only added, never deleted.
  • ⭐ Lifetime points only go upPoints you spend are recorded separately and never lower your lifetime total or level.
  • 🎁 Spend your balanceAt Grace Rock events and the Good Points shop. No cash value.
  • 🔍 Check your recordAsk Grace any time with your volunteer ID.
