How it works

  • 1. PostFill in the form below; it goes to Grace on WhatsApp. Grace checks it, gives it a task number and puts it on the board.
  • 2. NotifyNew tasks go out to registered volunteers (GR-V), businesses (GR-S) and customers (GR-C).
  • 3. Take it onTap “I can do this” or “I know someone”, and Grace connects both sides.
  • 4. PointsOnce both sides confirm it’s done, Grace records the Good Points.

Good Points

  • Simple task +10About an hour, e.g. translating a short text or finding some information.
  • Medium task +20About half a day, e.g. going along to an appointment or finding the right provider.
  • Hard task +30A day or more, or needs professional qualifications, e.g. a business introduction.
  • Posting a need +5Given to the poster once the task is done.

Same scale as volunteer Good Points: per task, not per hour. Each task is capped at +30, however big the deal. Grace reviews hard tasks before points are given. Good Points can’t be cashed out; any business commission is agreed separately and disclosed.
