CALD Youth Collective 2027 📍 Melbourne VIC

⏰ EOI closes Fri 20 Nov 2026 · 8 places

headspace Syndal is recruiting 8 young volunteers for its 2027 mental health & wellbeing projects for the Chinese community: share your ideas and experience, and help plan and run the projects. No experience needed. International students and new migrants are welcome!

📍 Note: this is a Melbourne program (Mount Waverley, VIC). Activities are in person at headspace Syndal. In Sydney? Please share it with friends in Melbourne.

Poster: headspace Syndal. The QR code on the poster is the “Apply online” button below; just tap it on your phone.

Who can apply

  • 🎂 Aged 18–25
  • 🗣️ Fluent in Chinese and English
  • 🌏 International students, new migrants or Chinese-background youth
  • 📍 Able to attend in person at headspace Syndal (Mount Waverley)
  • ⏱️ At least 12 hours a year, or 6+ activities

What you get

  • 🩺 Free Mental Health First Aid (MHFA) training
  • 💼 Free CV and interview skills training
  • 🌱 Volunteer experience in mental health and multicultural community projects
  • 🎓 Volunteer certificate and thank-you gift
  • 🤲 Support from a professional team
