Christmas opening · Open call

Wear your finest traditional dress
and sing Auld Lang Syne

Sat 5 Dec · from 5:30pm · North Turramurra

The Glengarry Christmas Celebration opens with a Welcome to Country by an Aboriginal Elder. Then people of many cultures, each in the finest traditional dress of their own culture, sing Auld Lang Syne together. We are looking for 20–30 singers: any age from grandparents to little ones, on your own or as a family. The key is your finest dress! Every singer receives a small gift.

What we ask

  • Your finest traditional dress, elegant and dignifiedAs long as it is beautiful: hanfu, qipao, sari, hanbok, kimono, kilt and more. Every culture is welcome. Please dress with care; very plain or casual outfits can look untidy. Wear it with pride and spirit. If you don't have traditional attire (for example, many Australian friends), please wear your most beautiful formal outfit.
  • Choir members: no T-shirts, jeans or shortsRespect yourself and your culture, and wear it with confidence. Restrooms are available for changing on site. No dress code for the audience.
  • Practise Auld Lang Syne beforehandPractise with this version: YouTube practice video ›
  • Be on time and follow the teamPlease arrive before 5:30pm.
  • Under-18s come with a parentA parent or guardian must agree when registering.
