Multicultural Christmas Choir · Open call

Wear your traditional attire
and sing in Christmas

Sat 5 Dec · 5:30–6:30pm · North Turramurra

We are inviting 20–30 young people or parents to sing Jingle Bells together at the Glengarry Christmas Celebration, each in the neat traditional dress of their own culture. Every singer receives a small gift, and everyone is welcome to stay for the Christmas evening.
