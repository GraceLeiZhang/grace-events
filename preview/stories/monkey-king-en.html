14 years as the Monkey King, audience or not

He Lingduo, “Xiaosheng”, a young street performer from Lijiang, Yunnan

🐒
14 years · one golden staffHe loved the Monkey King as a child and has played him for 14 years

His story

Community · Inspiring
  • 🐒 A childhood heroHe taught himself every move by copying performances on TV, and has kept going for 14 years.
  • 🏅 Award winnerHe won a Bronze Monkey award at a Monkey King competition.
  • 🎓 Performing his way through uniNow a third-year university student, he performs on the streets of Yunnan to pay his tuition and living costs.
  • ⏰ Eight hours a dayFrom afternoon until late at night, earning about 300 yuan a day. He hasn’t been home for Chinese New Year in three years.
  • 🎶 He finishes the showOften there is almost nobody watching, but once the music starts, he performs the whole routine.

In his words

“First, I love it. I’ve been doing this for 14 years. Second, if I don’t perform today, I don’t eat.”

To him, the audience is always there in his heart, so the show must go on.

Why we share it

Passion, persistence and standing on your own feet. Doing your best whether or not anyone is watching. That is what Grace Rock is about: every one of us, getting better.

If you meet this Monkey King on the streets of Yunnan, stop, watch and give him a round of applause 👏
