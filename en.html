Before you go

  1. Photo ID is a must 🪪

    Driver licence, passport, Service NSW photo card or student card (12+). No photo ID, no play – no matter how good a shot you are.

  2. Parent waiver form

    Under-18s must have a waiver signed by a parent. If you won't be there on the day, please give the signed waiver to Grace in advance – kids can't enter without a parent's signature.
    Download the waiver (Downloads page on the official site)

  3. Under 18

    On the day, Grace will sign in on site as the accompanying adult (18+) leading the group – this is a group-leader sign-in, not a guardian signature. Parents don't need to provide a copy of their driver licence, but kids still need the parent-signed waiver to enter.

  4. What to wear

    Comfortable clothes and sneakers; no open-toed shoes. If you wear large-framed glasses, contacts are recommended. For extra protection, bring a vest, gloves and a neck cover.

  5. Extra paintballs (buy on site if needed after you start)

    Before the day, have a chat with your kids about tactics: aim before you shoot, and don't go trigger-happy from the start. 100 balls go fast, and if you spray wildly, no amount of money will be enough. That's what we've learnt from many outdoor field battles at Delta Force Canberra since 2018 😅
    $22 = 100 · $190 = 1,000 · $370 = 2,000 · $535 = 3,000 · $800 = 5,000

  6. Food and parking

    No outside food or drinks, so eat before you play – and no sneaking snacks onto the battlefield. Customer parking is on the left of the building: enter through the black gate and park on the right.
