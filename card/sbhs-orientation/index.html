How it works · 怎么参与

  1. Add your name and amount in the next empty row (suggested $20 per family, any amount is welcome). 填名字和金额，建议每家 $20，多少都可以。
  2. Pay by PayID below, reference “SBHS + your name”. 转账备注“SBHS + 名字”。
  3. Grace orders from Costco, delivered to the school canteen where possible, and buys the fresh fruit herself on Mon 7 Dec so it's fresh. Costco 能送的直接送到学校；新鲜水果 12 月 7 日周一傍晚亲自去买，保证新鲜。
  4. Receipts are shared here afterwards. Any balance goes to the SBHS P&C. 事后公布小票，余款全部捐给 P&C。

Please chip in by Mon 30 Nov so the Costco order arrives in time. 请在 11 月 30 日前参与，方便提前下单。
